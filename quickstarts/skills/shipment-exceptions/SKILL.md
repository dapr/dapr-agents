# Shipment exception routing

Use this skill when a shipment is delayed and may miss its delivery window.

1. Activate the skill and read `references/exception-routing-policy.md`.
2. Match the shipment facts to the most specific policy route.
3. Report the selected route and the evidence from the policy.

The policy is advisory. Do not call external logistics systems, execute code, or mutate shipment data.
